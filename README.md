## Hi, I’m Majid

I like turning interesting AI ideas into useful software. My work sits between **research and implementation**—training models, building inference workflows, exposing them through APIs, and making them usable in real products.

I’m especially interested in **computer vision**, **generative AI**, and **large language models**. I’m open to thoughtful collaborations, research projects, and freelance work.

## What I work on

- **Computer vision:** image classification, object detection, segmentation, face recognition, and real-time video systems
- **Deep learning:** CNNs, RNNs, transformers, model optimization, and multi-modal applications
- **Generative AI:** LLM applications, RAG pipelines, synthetic data, voice technologies, and AI-powered content creation
